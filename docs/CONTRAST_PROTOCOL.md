# Complementary claims: prospective measurement and channel-transfer protocol

20 November 2026. This protocol is written before the new complementary-claim
captures. It is a bounded neutral validation experiment, not a promise to keep
changing methods until a desirable test score appears. All prespecified methods
and failed conditions must remain visible.

## Why this follows the question comparison

The previous queried sender readout gets24/32 on four new wording groups, while
the original story readout gets16/32 and unchanged queried-to-story transfer
gets16/32. Those data are now observed development evidence.

A separately recorded post-hoc saved-array diagnosis does not rescue the story
readout by simple within-template centering. Fresh sender within-template AUC
averages0.3125, and three declared centering/contrast diagnostics get12/32,
10/32 and10/32. This rules out a simple offset-only explanation for that frozen
readout. Factorial decomposition also shows fragile alignment of its nested
world-by-recorded-access interaction. None of this establishes absence of belief
information in the model.

The next intervention is to vary the candidate proposition itself. For the same
scene, capture both "token in A" and "token in B" separately. The candidates are
mutually exclusive and exhaustive under the authored two-location rules. They
remain statements to assess, not evidence delivered to either character.

## Fixed methods and endpoints

Both inputs stop before an answer. Conditions are story-plus-candidate and the
same input with the original explicit nested question. Block16 is primary;
blocks8 and24 are captured and archived without fresh-guided layer selection.
The checkpoint and NF4/BF16 runtime remain unchanged.

There are two distinct declared endpoints; neither replaces the other:

1. **Frozen measurement diagnostic:** apply the original saved single-claim head
   with its exact scaler, per-row L2 normalization and orientation, then subtract
   raw margins for B minus A. Its fitted intercept cancels. Its nonlinear row
   normalization means common raw scene components need not cancel exactly.
2. **New neutral readout:** fit a logistic head on the raw activation difference
   B minus A, using a training-only feature scale, no per-row L2 normalization
   and no fitted intercept. Sign-augment training with the reversed pair and
   complementary label. Choose C only on legacy validation loss from the fixed
   original grid. This head is linear in the two activation vectors.

Train-only mean subtraction applied identically to A and B cancels in the
difference. If representations contained only additive world, receipt and
candidate components, the new linear head could not externally manufacture
their nested interaction. Successful prediction would support an extractable
scene-candidate relation, not a demonstrated causal or spontaneous belief code.

Comparators are the unchanged original single-claim head, a matched newly fitted
single-claim head, single and paired TF-IDF, candidate-only structural controls,
and an oracle world-copy diagnostic. Separate world, actual-reader and nested
heads expose competing targets. TF-IDF is a lexical comparator, not a capable
independent semantic monitor. No inversion of orientation after viewing results
is allowed. No nonlinear activation classifier, layer sweep, test-set centering
or representation alignment is part of this run.

## Training and new validation materials

Legacy source panels remain immutable. The original four training groups alone
fit models; the original two validation groups choose C. Previously observed
test and fresh-query groups are reported as development data, never as fresh
confirmation. Delivered-current controls never fit or select a setting.

The new bank has128cases: eight new wording/entity groups in each of two
channels, each exhausting all eight world/actual-access/sender-record cells.
Channel one uses observation of a move. Channel two uses actual delivery of a
trusted current-location message and a receipt trusted by the sender. Actual
delivery and the sender's receipt vary independently. A false-positive receipt
is possible, and is not known to be unreliable by the sender.

The sender view omits actual private access. Its identical-input attendance or
delivery pairs have opposing actual-reader labels, so that target must score
exactly50%. The nested label instead follows the sender's trusted record. When
the record says no update occurred, the attributed reader retains the original
location even when current world truth differs. All labels are normative
fictional states; they are not measurements of hidden LLM beliefs.

The new message channel changes the evidence operation but retains the same
Boolean target rule. Describe this as **evidence-channel transfer**, not broad
reasoning-family OOD. New entities, wording and input bytes must be disjoint
from all development cases. Materials are agent-assisted and require human
semantic review; independent agent review is not a replacement for that review.

All methods, transforms, coefficients, vocabulary and selection metadata freeze
and receive hashes before any fresh forward pass. Fresh evaluation loads those
artifacts and performs no fitting or selection.

## What counts as evidence

Score one B-target prediction per case, view, condition and method. The A-target
prediction is its complement; counting both as independent evidence would double
the denominator artificially. Hidden-access duplicates and logical cells are
repeated measurements. Report results by wording group and channel, class recall,
confusion, loss and fixed-orientation AUROC.

For the new story/sender/nested/block16 paired head, the development progression
gate requires, separately in both new channels:

- accuracy at least0.75;
- recall at least0.60 for each class;
- negative log loss below log(2);
- every wording group at least0.50 accuracy.

These are predeclared engineering/research progression criteria, not a
significance test or a publication threshold. If they fail, preserve and report
the failure; do not select a different fresh-scoring method as the primary.

Inspect nested-vs-world and nested-vs-actual-reader disagreement cells. Changes
in the sender's trusted record should change nested targets; changes only in
private actual access should not. Simultaneously reversing world and trusted
record preserves the nested target. A/B score antisymmetry is enforced by the
method and is not an empirical capability result.

Only after a matching neutral readout passes independent validation should a
new pressure-transfer claim be attempted. Prior attribution, known-message
updating and unknown-future-message forecasting remain separate targets. The
Apollo/MASK and natural-report work is preserved; this diagnostic does not
establish deception detection or finish the main study.
