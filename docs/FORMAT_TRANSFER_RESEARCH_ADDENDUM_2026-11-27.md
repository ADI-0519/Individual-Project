## Format and label validity already have direct controls

Devbunova, [Is Evaluation Awareness Just Format Sensitivity?](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19426v1),
v1, 19 March 2026. Read the full main HTML text, Sections 1–6, and Appendix
A.1–A.6, including training protocols, preprocessing, layer tables and rewrite
instructions.

The study crosses evaluation/deployment proxies with structured/conversational
inputs using Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct in BF16. It matches the length distribution
of the original contrast, checks rewrite answer leakage, and contrasts ordinary
with pooled decorrelated fitting. At layer 24, benchmark-formatted deployment
false positives fall from 94.5% to 7.2% under the latter procedure. Appendix A.4
uses training-split StandardScaler and logistic regression with C=1.

This is further direct precedent for format-sensitive probes and training-based
repair. Its pooled 80/20 evaluation is distinct from the original zero-shot
contrast; the text does not establish equal fitting counts or an intercept-only
comparator. Context labels also depend on dataset proxies, explicitly including
first-turn MuTual rather than its complete conversational task. Successful
classification therefore remains conditional on that operational target. Our
fresh-context allocation and paired formal states answer a narrower question;
they do not make format fragility itself novel.

## Equal current answers do not establish equal updating

Li et al., [Same Answer, Different Future](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.07913v1),
v1, 5 November 2026. Read Sections 1–8 and limitations; Appendices A–H and J–K;
the start of I and L. The remaining prompt inventory was not exhaustively read.

They compare probabilistic Bernoulli evidence delivered as records, summaries,
assertions or instructions, then append identical further evidence. Their
continuation and numerical-report channels can disagree, and states matched
within 0.02 in current estimates can update differently. Appendix J retains
nonseparated comparisons. Appendix C uses family bootstrap and a readout-mass
qualification; H derives equivalence tolerances from wording variation.

Their hidden-state writes and continued activation injection concern the model's
behavior, not fitting a classifier intercept. The latter is our Offset control.
The paper's evidence-re-reading explanation remains bounded: K's attention
ablation reduces readable content and weight together, rather than uniquely
localizing weight. This is strong adjacent precedent for query/format-dependent
belief measurements and for testing future behavior. It does not evaluate our
fictional sender-attributed receipt target, equal-budget factor-readout arms or
complete receipt quartets.

## Consequence for the current study

Keep the claim tied to the measured comparison: whether allocating the same
512 fitting observations across A and B improves linked correction judgments,
and how that procedure compares with an intercept adjustment given the same
observations. Mixed versus Offset cannot uniquely identify a rotation, since
the procedures also differ in joint fitting versus freezing a finite-sample
A-half direction. Two fixed formats do not establish arbitrary format robustness.

The [input-only independent audit](validation/2026-11-27-format-adaptation-leakage-review-v1.json)
checks the new allocation and prefix isolation. The
[old-data relevance analysis](results/logical-relevance-old-data-2026-11-27/README.md)
separately shows why ingredient accuracy and complete-judgment accuracy must be
reported together. Neither check measures spontaneous anticipation or causal
use of a belief algorithm. The fresh experiment must finish with its primary,
all controls and independent numerical audit intact, regardless of its score.
