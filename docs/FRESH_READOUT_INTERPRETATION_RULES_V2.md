# How to interpret the fresh study

22 November 2026. Written during training/development capture, before any fresh
test capture or score. These interpretation limits supplement the unchanged
[execution protocol](FRESH_READOUT_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL_V2.md); they introduce no
new method, endpoint, threshold or model selection.

## What the primary comparison identifies

S0 and S1 use the same source fitting rows, development rows, fitting budget
and classifier family. S0 fixes the original capture site; S1 selects a site
using source development loss. Their fresh hard-correction difference tests
the transfer of that particular selection procedure. It is not the maximum
information available anywhere in the model. The twelve source-site results
are descriptive controls; an attractive test site cannot replace S1.

The selection rule may choose the original site and exactly the same head.
If S0 and S1 are identical, their primary difference is structurally zero.
Report that collapsed comparison explicitly. A zero interval then supplies no
independent evidence that different capture locations perform equivalently.

| Observed outcome | Supported reading | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Different S1 improves over S0 | The frozen source-only selection procedure improves this held-out comparison. | Do not generalize to all shifts, all sites or spontaneous anticipation. |
| Different S1 fails to improve | This selection procedure does not establish a transfer benefit on the fixed cohort. | Information may remain accessible to other readouts or sites. |
| C1 improves over source-trained S1 | Direct correction supervision changes accessibility/generalization at the same site and row budget. | The fitting mechanisms and number of narrative groups differ. |
| C2 is strong while C1 is weak | Four ingredients are accessible to learned readouts, and a supplied rule combines them successfully. | Four labels and external computation are privileged; this does not reveal the model's own algorithm. |

## Controls that qualify the result

Show one-step, easy-correction and directly asserted attributions alongside
hard corrections, in both renderers and models. If the easier controls are weak,
a hard-correction failure cannot be isolated as a composition-specific deficit.
Retain world/name changes and receipt-changing/preserving pairs: high row
accuracy alone does not demonstrate the intended dependence or invariance.
C2's factor labels are validated only for the two-message correction conditions.
Its other verdicts are pipeline transfer controls, not measurements of factor
accuracy on a valid one-step or directly asserted factor decomposition.

The output comparator uses the final prefix's conditional YES/NO logits. Its
probability is normalized over two tokens, even when neither is the model's
full-vocabulary first choice. Read it alongside the strict first-token
diagnostic, candidate-token mass and full-denominator accuracy. These first-token
checks do not replace a free-generation behavioral evaluation. Earlier capture
positions also have less context than the final output position.

## Uncertainty, replication and construct

The primary contains 192 complete four-case panels but only 24 paired narrative
units. The fixed bootstrap resamples those whole contexts. Its interval is
conditional on the fitted models, chosen sites, authored cohort, two renderers
and NF4 runtime; it does not include uncertainty from retraining or population
sampling. Both checkpoints use the same contexts. Do not turn them into 48
independent units or promote Qwen to the primary based on its outcome.

Report raw orientation, failures and all denominators. Calibration diagnostics
are unrecalibrated; do not repair a test reversal or tune thresholds after
inspection. A numerical audit establishes agreement with saved data under its
checks, not proof that every scientific assumption is correct.

The operational target is a fictional sender's attributed current reader belief
after an explicit question. Decoding it does not establish private mental
states, deceptive intent, causal use or spontaneous communicative anticipation.
This is a controlled readout-reliability study relevant to Variant 2's extension.
The original [8B Apollo adaptation](APOLLO_METHOD.md) and
[MASK pilot](MASK_SPECIFICITY_RESULTS.md) remain separate evidence; this study
does not reproduce Apollo's 70B deception-monitoring results.

The [primary-paper evidence map](MAIN_STUDY_RESEARCH_EVIDENCE_MAP_2026-11-22.md)
records existing belief mechanisms, token/layer effects, selectivity controls
and composition tests. The prospective contribution is the bounded comparison
and its controls, not first discovery of second-order belief. Agent semantic
review remains distinct from independent human review. Neither publication,
marks nor admissions outcomes can be inferred from an attractive score alone.
